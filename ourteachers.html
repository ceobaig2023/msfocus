How we teach

Explain first

Every topic starts with the idea behind it, in plain language, before any formula or answer is memorised.

Practise often

Regular practice, practicals and short tests show students where they stand and what to fix.

Make it fun

Activities, experiments and technology keep students curious. Learn and fun go together.
