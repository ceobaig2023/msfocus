About Focus Institute

Learn and fun, from Class 1 to Intermediate.

Focus Institute in Malakpet, Hyderabad, helps students master their subjects with clear explanations, regular practice and practical work. Alongside the syllabus, students meet STEM and robotics, so ideas move from the page to something they can build and test.

Mr. Mirza Mohammed Baig leads the institute, and Ms. Ayesha Mehreen has served as its principal. Call or message us for current batches, timings and admissions.
